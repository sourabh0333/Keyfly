## Competitors' pricing pages (2026-11-06)

All five follow the same template: plan cards, a monthly/yearly toggle, a comparison table, a FAQ. Their weak spots are where KeyFly can win:

- **Main competitor:** four cards with a first-month price and an **unlabelled price range** ($10–$8); a "save up to 61%" claim nothing on the page supports; usage as a multiplier ("2.5x") with no base unit; **no free plan on the cards** (only in the FAQ); no top-ups; no refund policy; no team plan.
- **Descript:** monthly and yearly prices shown without labels; "media hours" vs "media minutes" and "per person" vs "per editor"; AI credits undefined on the cards; Free buried below Enterprise; top-ups mentioned without prices.
- **Runway:** the clearest: credits translated into output ("625 credits = 52 s of Gen-4.5"), tabs for Individual / Team & Enterprise / API, an honest 20% yearly toggle; rollover differs by plan.
- **VEED:** AI credits don't roll over; more only by upgrading; per-seat costs unexplained.
- **OpusClip:** a value calculator with unexplained outputs; credits never defined.
- **HeyGen:** credits with no conversion; yearly prices only in the FAQ; conflicting rollover rules.

**What wins:** one defined unit (AI minutes, counted only while the AI works) shown before it runs; every price labelled (monthly or yearly, what's billed); the free plan up front; the cheapest way in to "no limits" (BYO, using your own AI); priced top-ups; a trial with no card and nothing locked after; projects that never expire. **Never name the competitor on the site.**
